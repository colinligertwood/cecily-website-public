A clear head, and time for what matters.

Cecily Tasks holds everything that has your attention, so your head doesn’t have to. Write it down, decide what it is, and Cecily brings it back on the right day.

Coming soon

For Mac and iPhone.

What it does for you.

Capture it in a moment.

Jot something down from anywhere on your Mac with a keyboard shortcut, or share a link or file from any app. Type “fri” or “tomorrow” and it lands on the right day.

An Inbox for deciding.

What you capture waits in the Inbox until you decide what it is: something to do, something to wait for, something for someday, or something to keep.

The right day, every day.

Agenda lays out today, the next six days, next week and the months ahead, with your calendar’s events alongside.

A weekly review, step by step.

Cecily walks you through your lists each week, one at a time, and shows the actions that haven’t moved in two weeks.

Waiting, with follow-ups.

Keep track of what others owe you, by person, with a follow-up date and a notification when it comes round.

Projects, one step at a time.

Break a task into subtasks, and Cecily points out any project with no next step.

How it works.

1

Capture

Get it out of your head and into the Inbox, the moment it occurs to you.

2

Decide

Give each item its place: an action, a person to wait on, someday, or reference, and a day if it has one.

3

Review

Once a week, go through your lists and choose what comes next. Then let it go.
