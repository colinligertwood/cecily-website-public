Use one, or all three.

CECILY

Budget

Money worries, put to bed.

Tell Cecily Budget what comes in each payday and what the household needs, and it plans where every dollar goes: the bills, the savings, the holiday fund. Each account is ready for what it has to pay, before the bank tells you otherwise.

More about Budget →

CECILY

Cards

Always square with the bank.

Cecily Cards reads your credit card statements and matches every purchase with the payment that covered it. Anything left unpaid, or paid twice, is flagged in plain sight. The statement holds no surprises.

More about Cards →

CECILY

Tasks

A clear head, and time for what matters.

Everything on your mind, written down in one place. Cecily Tasks brings each thing back on the right day and walks you through a quiet review each week, so nothing is carried in your head.
