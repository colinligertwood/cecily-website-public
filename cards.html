What it does for you.

Every payment, matched.

Auto-Match pairs each payment with its purchases: one for one, or one payment covering several.

Near misses, caught.

For anything left over, Cecily suggests likely partners, such as a purchase with a tip added after you paid.

Nothing slips through.

Purchases left unpaid and payments Cecily can’t place wait for your review. Mark each one paid, pay later, disputed or ignored.

A reminder when it’s time to pay.

Pay later adds a reminder to your Reminders app for the day you choose, and ticks it off once the purchase is paid.

Note it before the statement does.

Write down a purchase or payment as it happens. When the real one arrives, Cecily matches it and moves your note across.

See where the money goes.

Spending by month, category and vendor, with recurring charges found for you.
